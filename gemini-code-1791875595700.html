How fast can you dispatch an engineer for emergency break-fix?

Our Centralized 24/7 Service Desk acknowledges incoming tickets in under 30 minutes. For critical sites in major metropolitan hubs across 100+ countries, emergency on-site arrival SLAs range from 2 to 4 hours.

Are field dispatches 100% white-labeled for SIs and MSPs?

Yes. Our engineers operate strictly under signed Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). They arrive at end-user locations in unbranded professional attire or wearing your co-branded digital credentials, representing your brand seamlessly.

What tools and access gear do engineers bring on-site?

Every technician arrives equipped with standard network diagnostics (Fluke copper/fiber testers, console rollover cables, hand tools). Specialized requirements—such as fiberglass safety ladders, step-stools, OTDRs, or personal service vans—are specified and dispatched during work order booking.

Do engineers speak fluent English for remote NOC bridge calls?

Yes. Every engineer in our 5,000+ global pool possesses dual-language capability: native local language fluency to interface with site host contacts, plus fluent professional English for real-time console bridge calls with your remote NOC/SOC team.

Terms of Service & Enterprise SLA Framework

Effective Date: October 2026 | Company: Jasper IT Solutions Ltd

1. Scope of Engagement

Jasper IT Solutions Ltd provides professional white-label IT field engineering, data center smart hands, structured cabling, and secure UK warehousing staging to System Integrators (SIs) and Managed Service Providers (MSPs).

2. Service Level Agreements (SLAs) & Client-Caused Delays

Emergency arrival SLAs are calculated strictly from the time of ticket acknowledgment by our 24/7 Service Desk. In the event of Client-Caused Delays—including delayed facility access clearances, missing replacement hardware, or site host unavailability—the active SLA clock safely pauses until site access or required materials are fully provided.

3. Hardware Handling & Insurance Liability

All on-site hardware replacements and staging are covered under comprehensive professional indemnity and public liability insurance. Liability for customer-owned hardware inside colocation facilities is governed strictly by agreed Master Services Agreements (MSAs).

White-Label NDA & Business Code of Conduct

Jasper IT Solutions operates strictly as an invisible back-end partner. Our Business Code of Conduct guarantees absolute brand protection for our enterprise clients.

1. Perpetual Non-Circumvention

All deployed engineers are bound by perpetual non-circumvention covenants. Field representatives are strictly prohibited from soliciting, contacting, or contracting directly with end-user client sites introduced through Jasper dispatches.

2. Brand & Documentation Sanity

Field engineers wear unbranded professional attire or client-provided badges and utilize sanitized documentation templates, guaranteeing absolute brand protection on-site.

3. Business Ethics & Anti-Corruption

Jasper IT Solutions enforces zero-tolerance policies regarding bribery, kickbacks, or unethical gifts. All technicians undergo rigorous background and compliance screenings before stepping on-site.

Health & Safety Policy

The safety and well-being of our engineers, client personnel, and site hosts are paramount across all global operations.

1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) & Compliance

All field engineers are mandated to adhere strictly to local facility safety regulations, wearing steel-toe footwear, high-visibility vests, protective eyewear, and specialized PPE as required inside data centers and construction sites.

2. Equipment Safety & Height Guidelines

Fiberglass safety ladders, step-stools, and lifting equipment are inspected regularly and dispatched in accordance with rigorous occupational safety standards to eliminate overhead or electrical hazards.

Privacy Policy & GDPR Data Security

Jasper IT Solutions Ltd is fully committed to upholding UK GDPR compliance and protecting sensitive operational data shared by enterprise SIs and MSPs.

1. Site Access & Credential Handling

All physical security badges, colocation clearance IDs, and site contact lists submitted for dispatch are encrypted in transit and securely purged from active technician devices within 48 hours of work order completion.

2. Operational Telemetry Logs

Ticket metadata and diagnostic logs are retained strictly for auditing and quality assurance under enterprise security protocols, never shared with third parties.

Legal Dispute Resolution & Governing Law

1. Executive Negotiation & Escalation

Both parties agree to first attempt resolution in good faith via direct negotiation between designated Senior Executive Directors within thirty (30) written notice days of a dispute arising.
