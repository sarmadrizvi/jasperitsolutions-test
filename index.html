Frequently Asked Questions & Escalation SLA

How fast can you dispatch an engineer for emergency break-fix?

Our Centralized 24/7 Service Desk acknowledges incoming tickets in under 30 minutes. For critical sites in major metropolitan hubs across 100+ countries, emergency on-site arrival SLAs range from 2 to 4 hours.

Are field dispatches 100% white-labeled for SIs and MSPs?

Yes. Our engineers operate strictly under signed Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). They arrive at end-user locations in unbranded professional attire or wearing your co-branded digital credentials, representing your brand seamlessly.

What tools and access gear do engineers bring on-site?

Every technician arrives equipped with standard network diagnostics (Fluke copper/fiber testers, console rollover cables, hand tools). Specialized requirements—such as fiberglass safety ladders, step-stools, OTDRs, or personal service vans—are specified and dispatched during work order booking.

Do engineers speak fluent English for remote NOC bridge calls?

Yes. Every engineer in our 5,000+ global pool possesses dual-language capability: native local language fluency to interface with site host contacts, plus fluent professional English for real-time console bridge calls with your remote NOC/SOC team.

How Are On-Site Escalations Handled?

In the rare event of on-site technical blockers, failed hardware replacements, or unexpected access delays, Jasper IT Solutions Ltd enforces a rigid, time-bound escalation procedure managed by our 24/7 Duty Operations Directors.

  • T + 15m Tier-3 Network Architect Direct Escalation
  • T + 30m Duty Operations Director & Client Incident Alert
